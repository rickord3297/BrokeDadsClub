Gear advice online is usually a wishlist dressed up as wisdom. These guides are for the dad who needs a tool that lasts, a thrift find that does not look like a costume, or a first soccer season that does not require the full catalog.

Buy what you will use. Borrow or wait on the rest. The point is not a perfect kit. It is a kit that matches this season of life and this paycheck.
