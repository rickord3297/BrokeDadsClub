Action Network's 2026 fan-cost math puts a family of four at an NFL game around **$1,475** on average: four tickets, hot dogs, sodas, beers for the adults, parking. Some buildings clear $1,800. That is not a casual afternoon. That is a weekend trip with a kickoff time.

If your kid asked "can we go" after the opener, you are not cheap for pausing. You are doing the same triage as [birthday party math](/guides/birthday-party-math) and [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go): one number, then a decision.

## Do this before you buy

1. **Price the real day.** Four cheapest available seats + parking + food you will actually buy. Not the fantasy "we will pack sandwiches and never pee."
2. **Compare to something else you want.** One Disney payment. One month of youth sports. Two months of streaming. See [NFL Sunday without a $1,000 stream](/guides/nfl-sunday-without-a-thousand-dollar-stream).
3. **Pick yes, not yet, or no.** Write it down. Guilt thrives in "maybe."

> Truth: One planned stadium day beats a season of half-promises.

## When the stadium is worth it

- You already saved for it (cash, not "put it on the card and figure it out")
- It is a milestone: first game, birthday, visiting grandparents in town
- You can eat before you go and set a hard merch rule ($0 or one shared souvenir)

## When the couch wins

- The cheapest four seats still wipe the emergency fund
- You would skip a bill to go
- The kid mostly wants the jersey photo, not three hours of commercials and bathroom lines

**Living-room substitute that still feels like an event:** team colors on, antenna or the one app you already pay for, pizza at kickoff, halftime backyard throws. Same Sunday. Different invoice.

## Baseball is the cheaper sports day

September baseball is still on. Family packs and value nights (ticket + dog + soda bundles in the $30-$70 per person range at some parks) are a different sport and a different receipt. If the craving is "take the kids to a real stadium," price a ballpark series night before you finance NFL nosebleeds.

College games and high-school Friday nights scratch the crowd itch for parking-lot money, not resort money.

## The point

An NFL Sunday with your kids can be magic. It can also be a second vacation you did not plan. Price the day. Pick yes or no out loud. Broke does not mean you never see a stadium. It means you refuse to pretend $1,475 is "just tickets."
