The group chat lights up on your partner's phone. Field trip form. Early release. Conference slot. You find out at dinner, already late, already annoyed.

Schools are not evil. They are overloaded. Whoever filled out the paperwork first often becomes "the parent" in the system. Dads get treated like the plus-one at their own kid's education. Reddit is full of single fathers whose teachers talk to a girlfriend or skip them at conferences entirely. You can fix the plumbing without a speech about gender politics in the parking lot.

## Do this today

1. **Email the school office.** Subject: "Add second parent contact for [child name]." Include your full name, phone, email, and child's grade/teacher.
2. **Log into the parent portal** with your own account if the school allows two guardians. See [the parent portal in five minutes a week](/guides/the-parent-portal-in-five-minutes-a-week).
3. **Pick one event to attend in person** this month. Back-to-school night, a game, one conference. Face beats forwarding.

> Remember: You are not asking permission to parent. You are updating a contact form.

## The conference line

Teacher turns to your partner. You are standing right there.

> "I am [name], [kid]'s dad. I need the updates sent to me too. Can we run through the same points?"

Not angry. Not sarcastic. Clear. If they apologize and adjust, move on. If they keep dodging, follow up in writing so there is a record.

> "Thanks for meeting today. Please copy me on future emails about [child]. My address is [email]."

Paper trail. Boring. Effective.

## Scripts with your co-parent

Partner: "I will just forward you stuff."

You:

> "I want to be on the school's list directly. Forwarding means I am always last."

Partner: "They always call me anyway."

You:

> "Then we tell them together that both of us are contacts. I am not your assistant for school."

If you are solo parenting, the fix is the same: office email, portal login, one in-person hello so the teacher maps your face to your kid.

## Show up without performing

You do not need to volunteer for every committee. You need one visible repeat:

- Same pickup spot twice a week
- One email that asks a specific question
- One event where you introduce yourself by name

Lonely at the fence? [The lonely dad at practice](/guides/the-lonely-dad) is the emotional side. This is the logistics side.

> "I am still learning the system. Point me at anything I need to sign."

People relax when they know you are not lost on purpose.

## What not to do

- Do not make your kid the messenger between adults.
- Do not argue with the teacher in front of your child.
- Do not ghost school because the system annoyed you once.
- Do not wait for your partner to "give you access" like it is their job to grant it.

## The point

Default parent is a database field, not a verdict on who cares more. Update the list, show up once, correct calmly when someone skips you. The school will not fix it until you insist once, in writing, without shame.
