The email subject line said "Welcome to the team!" The attachment said $85 registration, $40 uniform deposit, $25 volunteer buyout, and "spirit pack strongly encouraged."

You did not budget wrong. You were given the trailer, not the movie.

Late August and September are sports signup season. Same rhythm as [the second bill after school starts](/guides/the-second-bill): the first number is never the last number.

**Before you click pay:** ask three questions below. Two minutes now beats a surprise fee in week three.

## Do this before you sign

Ask in email or at the table:

1. **All-in range:** "What did most families spend last season total?"
2. **Hard stops:** "What is required vs optional?"
3. **Help:** "Is there scholarship gear, loaner equipment, or a payment plan?"

Leagues have heard this before. Silence is data.

Use the [school supply triage printable](/resources/school-supply-triage) if you are triaging the whole August bill stack at once.

## The fees that show up after "yes"

**Uniform and gear:** cleats, pads, special socks, the package deal that is cheaper than buying piecemeal but still hurts.

**Pictures and trophies:** optional until your kid is the only one without the package.

**Travel:** one tournament an hour away becomes gas, lunch, and a hotel you did not plan.

**Volunteer hours or buyout:** time you do not have, priced in dollars instead.

**Mid-season asks:** pizza money, coach gift, replacement mouthguard because this one lives in the mud.

None of this makes you a bad parent for noticing. It makes you the CFO the league forgot to cc.

## If it fits (barely)

- Pay registration first, spread gear over two paychecks if allowed.
- Buy used cleats from a parent one grade up. They outgrow before they wear out.
- Skip the spirit pack if the generic version at Target works. Your kid wants to play, not to model a logo.

Pair with [the $47 grocery week](/guides/the-47-dollar-grocery-week) if food and sports are fighting the same paycheck.

## If it does not fit

You are allowed to say no to a season. Not no to your kid forever. No to this bill this month.

Script:

- "We are not doing travel team this year. We are doing rec at the park."
- "One sport this fall. You pick."
- "Not this season. Here is what we can do instead: [free practice, different league, gym with us on Saturdays]."

More on the kid conversation in [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go).

## When you already said yes and the next fee hits

Pause before auto-pay. Text the coach or treasurer: "What is this for and is it required?"

Sometimes "strongly encouraged" means skip. Sometimes it means your kid cannot play without it. You need the truth, not the vibe.

## The point

Sports are good for a lot of kids. They are also a subscription service with jerseys. Naming the real cost early beats surprise in week three and keeps you from paying on a card you are still paying off from last season. Broke does not mean your kid never plays.
