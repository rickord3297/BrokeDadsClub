Cyber Monday is November 30. The toy tabs will still be open. That cart is already a plan: one name, then stop, in [the list is one name](/guides/the-list-is-one-name). This errand is the Santa chair. A lot of malls keep it through December 24, so Saturday is not the deadline.

Holyoke Mall posted the split on September 10. The visit is free. Photo packages start at $44.99. And if you lift your phone, they will tell you to put it down.

> Remember: The visit can be free. The package is a second cart.

## The visit and the file are different prices

Holyoke's set is run by Cherry Hill Programs, in Cafe Square, from November 20 through December 24. It is closed on Thanksgiving. Visits are free, reservations are encouraged, and walk-ups can still get in when there is room. The FAQ says they do not allow personal photography, because their photographer is the one taking the picture. At that mall, the file you keep is the one you pay for.

Cherry Hill Mall in New Jersey uses the same company and a different bill. Its page says a reserved time slot is $19.99, and that reservation does not require a package. Packages still start at $44.99. The $19.99 holds a place in line. It is not a digital file. Walk-ups are welcome when there is room, so a paid reservation is a choice. The set runs November 6 through December 24.

Montgomery Mall in North Wales, Pennsylvania, posts the single-file price. North Pole Photo lists one copyright-free digital at $24.95. Three digital images, three poses, is $37.95. The Family Pack is $59.95. Santa's Best is $79.95, and that box adds every digital they shoot plus 12 Christmas cards. One file to the pack is the whole jump. The set runs, on select dates, through December 24.

Macy's Herald Square is the same menu with a bigger sign. A general reservation is free and required. The set opens November 27. On Macy's FAQ, Package C is $44.99 for four printed photos. Package B is $49.99 and adds digital downloads. Package A is $54.99. If you want the file, the print-only package does not include it. The $250 Premium visit, for up to eight guests, is already sold out. A free reservation can still mean a 30 to 90 minute wait, and weekends run longer.

Your mall will not match all four. Open the page. Find the single digital. Write that number on a note. If the page only says packages start at $44.99, ask which package is one file before anyone sits.

> Truth: One pose is the photo. A stack of wallets is wrapping paper.

## Write the number, then sit

Do it in the parking lot, not at the counter.

If the sheet looks like Montgomery's, the number is $24.95. If the cheapest file is $49.99, that is the number. It is not $59.95 because the sheet shows wallets. It is not $79.95 because the top box includes cards you can make at the kitchen table.

Holyoke lets you lock a time and pick the package after the visit. Their FAQ calls it reserve now and decide later. Use that. Sit. Let your kid say one thing. Then buy the one file, or buy nothing. A package you pay for online is harder to walk back. Holyoke says a cancelled reservation is not refunded, and pre-purchased packages are final.

If a donation screen pops up after you pay, it is not part of the photo price. Skip it, or name a dollar before you tap.

A weekday is the shorter version of this stop. Holyoke on Monday, November 30, runs 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday, December 5, runs 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Montgomery's early December weekdays are 3 p.m. to 7 p.m., after school. Go when you can leave.

> To your kid: "You get one picture. Tell him one thing."

> To the counter: "One digital. Not the pack."

If the cheapest file is over the number on your note, you still saw Santa.

> Say: "We'll take the visit. No package today."

That is a complete outing. The kid asked to see him. The envelope was the store's idea.

## If the line wants more

Somebody in the stroller line will already have the big pack. Your kid will see a bag of prints. You do not have to match the bag.

> To your kid: "We got one picture. That one goes on the fridge."

If there is no picture, say that in the same breath. Do not apologize in the food court.

> Say: "We saw Santa. The picture can wait."

A short no is the same muscle as [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go). A photo package does not come out of the gift name, and the gift name does not become a reason to add wallets.

> Remember: One file, then you leave. December 24 is the last chair, not an upgrade deadline.

## The point

Broke doesn't mean broken. Check your mall's page before you go. Write one price for one digital pose. If they ban your phone, that file is the picture. If the number is too high, take the visit and skip the envelope. The kid met Santa. That was the ask.
