Kickoff is this weekend. The ads want you to stack five apps, add Sunday Ticket, and call it "just this season." That is how football becomes a fourth streaming bill next to the ones you already resent.

You do not need every game. You need *your* game, maybe one night game, and enough dignity not to spend a thousand dollars to sit on your own couch.

## Do this before Sunday

1. **Write your team and market.** Local CBS/Fox game? Antenna. Done for that slot.
2. **List what you already pay for.** Prime, Peacock, YouTube TV, whatever is already on the credit card.
3. **Pick one add-on max** for this weekend if the game is not free. Cancel it Monday if you only needed one night.

> Remember: Watching everything is a product. Watching your team is a habit.

## The cheap stack that actually works

**Free / once:**
- Over-the-air antenna for local CBS, Fox, NBC. Hardware is $20-$60 once. Your home game is often here.

**By the month (buy only when you need it):**
- Amazon Prime for Thursday Night Football if you do not already have it
- Peacock for Sunday Night Football months you care about
- ESPN app month for Monday Night if that is your thing

**Usually skip:**
- NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV if you live in-market. It is built for people who moved away or want every slate at once. New-subscriber season pricing starts in the mid-hundreds; returning fans pay more. That is not "one more app." That is a second car payment for remote clickers.

RedZone is fun. RedZone is not free. Treat it like dessert, not dinner.

## Scripts for the living room

Kid: "Why can't we get every game?"

You:

> "We watch our team. The other games are for people who pay for every channel. We are not those people."

Partner: "Just get Sunday Ticket this year."

You:

> "Show me which games we miss without it. If it is one rivalry, we find a bar or a friend. We are not financing the whole league."

## If you already bought the full stack

Cancel what you can on Monday. Keep one primetime path. Antenna stays. No shame spiral. Same energy as [the dad tax](/guides/the-dad-tax): name the line item, then cut it.

A cheap Sunday still counts as a weekend. Pair the game with a backyard throw or a couch rule from [cheap weekend that is not just screens](/guides/cheap-weekend-not-just-screens) so the kids get one play break at halftime.

## The point

Football is not supposed to cost more than a weekend trip. Antenna first. One optional app. Cancel on Monday. Broke does not mean you sit in silence on Sunday. It means you refuse to rent the entire league to feel like a fan.
