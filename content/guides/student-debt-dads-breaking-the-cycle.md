You are 38. The loan payment still auto-drafts. Your kid asks about college like it is a theme park you forgot to mention the ticket price for.

This is not a moral failure. It is a math problem that got handed down. You can work your own number down and teach them a different script before the FAFSA season panic.

## Face your number (once)

Most dads know the payment. Fewer know the balance, the rate, or whether it is federal or private. Pull one servicer login. Write three things on a sticky note:

- Total balance
- Interest rate
- Minimum payment

That is it for tonight. No spreadsheet heroics.

> Remember: Shame lives in vague numbers. Ink kills the fog.

If you have Parent PLUS loans on top of your old undergrad debt, list them separately. Different rates, different rules, same paycheck.

## What you owe yourself

Your debt is not their homework. But hiding it teaches them that money is a secret, and secrets become surprise loans.

**Little kids:** keep it to the weather.

> "We pay for our home and food first. Some money goes to old school bills too."

**Teens:** be honest about the shape, not the horror.

> "I am still paying for my degree. That is why I care where you go and what it costs."

You are not asking them to fix your past. You are showing them the receipt.

## Break the cycle before junior year

The expensive mistake is starting the talk during campus tours. By then everyone is emotional and the sticker price feels fake.

**Start with net price, not the brochure.** Every school has a net price calculator on its site. Run three schools: one dream, one solid, one cheap. Write what you could actually pay per year without new debt.

**Name the two-year move.** Community college for gen eds, then transfer, is not a consolation prize. It is a $20,000-$40,000 discount. Say it out loud before they absorb the idea that anything less than a four-year dorm is failure.

> To your kid: "We are not anti-college. We are anti-surprise bills."

**Cap borrowing to a first-year salary.** A rough rule: total student debt at graduation should not beat what they might earn in year one in their field. English teacher? Different cap than software engineer. Google the starting range together. Let the number be boring.

**Say no to Parent PLUS unless you can carry it.** Your name, your payment, your retirement at risk. If you would not take a car loan for that amount on your income alone, do not sign for college either. There are cheaper schools. There is a gap year. There is working part-time locally.

## Small moves that compound

- **Auto-pay discount:** Many servicers shave 0.25% if you enroll. Takes five minutes.
- **Round up:** Pay $50 over minimum on your highest-rate loan only. Not spread across all of them.
- **Employer help:** Some jobs still offer student loan reimbursement. HR might not advertise it.
- **One family rule:** No new education debt for anyone without a written one-page plan: school, total cost, monthly payment, who pays.

Pair the kid talk with [talking to kids about money](/guides/talking-to-kids-about-money). When they ask for the expensive trip or gear, [explaining "we can't go"](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go) is the same muscle.

## The point

You cannot erase 2009. You can stop 2044 from rhyming with it.

> Truth: The best gift is not a perfect campus photo. It is a kid who knows what debt costs before they sign.

Broke does not mean broken. It means you read the fine print this time.
