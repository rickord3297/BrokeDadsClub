The class party email shows up while you are still figuring out the costume. A signup link. Cupcakes for 30. A fruit tray. Themed snacks. Plates. And a line that says they need parents in the room from 1:15 to 2:30.

You can help. You do not have to take the sheet.

> Remember: One slot is help. The whole signup is a second job.

## Decide the slot before you click

Open the link after you already know your answer. Drinks, a salty snack, or nothing new this week. "I'll see what's left" is how you end up with the bakery order and the afternoon.

A lot of schools want store-bought food with the label still on it, and nut-free if the note says so. That rule is useful. A sealed box from the grocery store counts. You are not failing a craft fair.

SignUpGenius's own class-party template is basically that list: about 30 store-bought cupcakes or cookies, one fruit tray, 30 snack bags, and 30 juice boxes or waters. Mid-October 2026, a 30-pouch box of Kool-Aid Jammers was $8.27 at Walmart. Capri Sun's 30-pouch box was listed around $9.47 to $9.67 the same week. That is a class of drinks. A themed bakery tray is a different purchase, and the sheet often wants 24 or 30 pieces.

> Say: "I can bring the juice boxes. I can't stay for the party."

If you can stay and you want to, stay. Let someone else bring the food. Two yeses is how a Friday afternoon becomes a Thursday night project.

## Skip the themed tray

Spider crackers and ghost pretzels photograph well. The kids eat the pretzels. Store-brand pretzels in the bag they came in still get eaten. Orange and black napkins are fine if that slot is actually empty and you already said yes to it. They are not a personality test.

The costume decision is a different bill. If you already picked one outfit for school, trunk-or-treat, and the 31st, this signup does not get to invent another look. That plan lives in [one Halloween costume, not three events](/guides/one-halloween-costume-not-three-events).

Same muscle as [not saying yes to team parent in the first huddle](/guides/dont-say-yes-to-team-parent-first-huddle). A team clipboard is a season. This is one party. The sentence is shorter, and it still works.

> Truth: Plain food that shows up beats a cute tray you resent.

## If you already clicked the big slot

You can narrow it the same day. The person running the party would rather have a sure box of drinks than a maybe on cupcakes, plates, and your afternoon.

> Say: "I grabbed too much. I can bring the drinks. I need to drop the rest."

Do it in the reply, not in a long apology. You are handing back slots someone else can take. That is helpful.

If money is the pinch, pick the cheapest named slot that is still sealed and allowed. Drinks and pretzels usually beat cupcakes. If every remaining slot is a bakery order, your job is the short no, not a speech about the budget. [The small asks that stack](/guides/dad-tax-examples-the-small-asks-that-stack) are the same shape: one yes feels tiny until it sits next to the costume.

Lunch packing is a different problem, and it already has a ceiling in [packing school lunch without a guilt spiral](/guides/packing-school-lunch-without-a-guilt-spiral). Do not let a class party reset that number for one Friday.

## What you tell your kid

They may want you in the room. That is a fair want. It is not the same as a debt.

> To your kid: "I'm sending the drinks. I have to work. Tell me one funny thing after."

If you are staying, say that too, and do not also promise the fancy snack on the way in. One job.

> Remember: Missing the party is not missing your kid.

## The point

Broke does not mean broken. Take one line on the sheet. Buy the plain pack on the way home. Leave the themed version in the aisle. Your kid still gets a party. You still get your afternoon.
