Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. If the car is the plan, the fare is already decided. Seats versus the drive is in [four seats or one car](/guides/four-seats-or-one-car). The decision left this week is the clock.

AAA published its Thanksgiving forecast on November 16, 2026. It expects 81.5 million people to travel at least 50 miles between Tuesday, November 24 and Monday, November 30. About 72.9 million of them are driving. INRIX, the traffic shop in that same release, says the busy outbound days are Tuesday and Wednesday. The way home piles up Sunday and Monday.

> Remember: Morning is the easy drive.

## Tuesday before 1, Wednesday before noon

You do not need a metro chart on the fridge. New York, Washington, Los Angeles, and Boston can see more than double a normal jam, and the INRIX table has the same shape almost everywhere. The bad hours are the afternoon and the early evening.

Tuesday, November 24: the worst window is 2 p.m. to 8 p.m. Leave before 1 p.m.

Wednesday, November 25: the worst window is noon to 7 p.m. Leave before noon.

A 3 p.m. start is the traffic. That is the hour after a full workday, after pickup, after one more email. It is also the hour the table says to miss.

> Truth: A 3 p.m. start is the traffic.

> Say: "We're leaving before noon."

Thursday, November 26, is marked minimal traffic. A Thursday morning start is a late lunch, and it is still the holiday. On the way home, Sunday, November 29, runs noon to 8 p.m. Leave before 11 a.m. Monday, November 30, runs 2 p.m. to 7 p.m. Leave before 1 p.m. Friday before noon works too.

## If Wednesday is still a school day

A full day until 3 means the before-noon window is gone. Tuesday before 1 p.m. is the drive, or you wait for Thursday morning. Both are real plans. Waiting on dismissal is how you enter the noon-to-7 window on purpose.

> Truth: A 3 p.m. dismissal is already the jam.

If work still wants a normal Tuesday, ask once.

> To work: "I need to leave before noon. Afternoon is the jam."

If the answer is no, use the window you still have: Thursday morning, Friday before noon, or the slow hours with the bag already packed.

## The bag is the dinner

AAA tied the small drop in drivers, about 200,000 fewer than last year, partly to record pump prices in August, September, and October. The national average on Thanksgiving Day has never been above $4 a gallon. The high was $3.58 in 2022. Leaving at 4 does not make the tank cheaper.

A jam turns hungry kids into a travel-plaza dinner. Pack the food the night before. The plain version is [gas station dinner](/guides/gas-station-dinner), already in the bag, so 6 p.m. is still just a slow highway.

> Truth: The delay can cost time. It does not have to cost a second dinner.

> To your kid: "Snacks are in the bag. We're not stopping for a meal."

Last Thanksgiving, AAA took more than half a million roadside calls for flats, dead batteries, fuel, lockouts, and tows. The night before, look at the tires and the fluids. That is a two-minute walk. It is not a reason to buy a new kit. If the spare is flat, handle it Tuesday morning, while you can still leave before 1.

Buy the pie before the car moves if you are a guest. That $5 or $6 job is in [the pie is the assignment](/guides/the-pie-is-the-assignment). If you are feeding the house, the list is already closed in [the basket is the dinner](/guides/the-basket-is-the-dinner). A bad departure time is a clock problem. The short no, if you are not going, is in [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go).

## What you tell the kids

They will ask why you are leaving while it is still morning.

> To your kid: "Morning is the easy road. Afternoon is the parking lot."

They need when the car moves, what is in the bag, and the fact that a slow highway is not an emergency. The 81.5 million is for you.

> Remember: You already decided to drive. The morning is the rest of it.

## Text the time tonight

Do this before the group thread picks 3 p.m. because that is when people get off.

- Tuesday: out before 1 p.m. Bags in the car the night before.
- Wednesday: out before noon. If school runs until 3, that window is gone. Use Tuesday, or Thursday morning.
- Coming home: Sunday before 11 a.m., or Monday before 1 p.m. Friday before noon works too.
- Food is packed. The plaza is not the dinner.
- Tires and fluids the night before. No new gear.

> Say: "Car leaves before noon. I'll text when we're on the road."

Broke does not mean broken. Leaving while the coffee is still warm is a real Thanksgiving drive. The 3 p.m. start is how a simple trip becomes the jam everybody already posted.
