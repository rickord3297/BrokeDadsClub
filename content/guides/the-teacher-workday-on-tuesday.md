Your phone buzzes Tuesday at 8 a.m. A parent in the group chat: "Enjoying the day off?"

Your stomach drops. It is a teacher workday. School is closed. Your job is not. You did not forget because you are careless. You forgot because the district calendar is a PDF from August that nobody reads like a bill.

> Remember: Teacher workdays are published early. The panic is optional.

## Workdays are not surprises

District calendars list professional development days, staff workdays, and random closures months ahead. They are boring. That is why they work.

Open the PDF once. Highlight every day students are off while you are not on PTO. Copy those dates to:

- Your phone calendar
- The fridge
- Whoever shares pickup with you

Ten minutes in August saves four frantic Tuesdays in fall.

This is the other half of [the daycare savings that never showed up](/guides/the-daycare-savings-that-never-showed-up): school-age care is not only after-school. It is scattered days off you stopped paying daycare to avoid.

## The cost of finding out Tuesday morning

Rec programs: $30-$80 if a spot exists. Drop-in sitter: $120-$200. PTO you did not bank: a performance conversation you did not need.

> Truth: A workday you planned costs time. A workday you forgot costs time and money.

## Three cover options ranked

**1. Parent rotation (cheapest):** Pair with one or two families. Each parent takes 2-3 workdays for the group. You use fewer PTO days than covering every closure alone.

**2. District rec / YMCA day camp:** Register when the calendar drops. Spots fill. $30-$80 beats panic pricing.

**3. WFH trade with your job:** Ask early, not at 8:02 a.m. Some managers say yes if you ask in August. Same manager says no on zero notice.

Pick per day. Not every workday needs the same plan.

## When you truly forgot

Call the usual backup: grandparent, neighbor, half-day WFH if allowed. Pay the sitter if you must. Then add the next workday to the fridge before bed.

Do not shame-spiral. Fix the calendar once.

## Scripts

> To your manager in August: "Here are four student days off this fall. I can WFH two and need coverage planning for two."

> To another parent: "Want to swap workdays? I'll take October if you take November."

> To yourself at 8 a.m. chaos: "Never again" means write it on the fridge tonight.

## Pair with the group chat

The [school group chat at 9 p.m.](/guides/the-school-group-chat-at-9-pm) will remind you the night before. That is backup, not a system. The district PDF is the system.

## The point

The teacher workday on Tuesday hurts because you treated the calendar like trivia. Pull the dates once, book cheap coverage or a parent swap, and stop learning about closures from a text that says "enjoying the day off." Broke does not mean broken. It means you read the boring PDF before it read you.
