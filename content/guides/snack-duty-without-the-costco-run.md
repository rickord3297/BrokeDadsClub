Snack signup sheet goes up in September. Your name lands on week four. Someone last year brought a folding table of organic pouches like it was a wedding catering bid.

You are not failing if you bring bananas and water. You are doing snack duty, not hosting a banquet.

> Remember: Kids want food and a ride home. They are not scoring your presentation.

## The $15 snack formula

One protein, one carb, water:

- **Protein:** cheese sticks, peanut butter crackers (check allergies), yogurt tubes on sale
- **Carb:** bananas, orange slices, store-brand pretzels
- **Water:** refill bottles from home if the team allows it

Skip the warehouse store unless you already shop there for [the grocery week](/guides/the-47-dollar-grocery-week). A special trip for snack duty is how $15 becomes $80 with a hot dog sample you did not need.

## Shop the kitchen first

Half a box of crackers. Apples from last week's cart. Freezer pops from July. Use them before you buy duplicates.

> Truth: Nobody audits whether the oranges came from Costco.

## Allergies and the one email

Before you buy peanut anything, read the team note or ask once:

> "Any allergies I should skip this week?"

One email beats an ER visit and a season of side-eye.

## When the signup feels like a flex contest

Some parents treat snacks like Instagram. That is their budget, not a rule.

Bring what you can afford. Show up on time. That is the job.

If you truly cannot swing the week, message the coach early:

> "I can do oranges and water, or swap weeks with someone."

Same dignity as [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go), shorter audience.

## Link to the real sports bill

Snack duty is the small line item on [the sports signup fee](/guides/the-sports-signup-fee-you-didnt-budget-for) invoice. Registration is fixed. Snacks are where you still have control.

Cap snack week in the same sinking fund as cleats and gas to practice.

## Scripts

> To the kid: "We're on snacks Thursday. Help me bag oranges."

> To another parent: "Keeping it simple this week."

> To yourself in the store: "Would I buy this if snack duty were not a performance?"

## The point

Snack duty without the Costco run is still snack duty. Protein, carb, water, allergies checked, budget capped. The kids will eat, the coach will thank you, and you will not fund a warehouse trip out of shame. Broke does not mean broken. It means oranges count.
