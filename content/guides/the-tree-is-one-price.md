Black Friday is when a lot of fresh-cut lots open. It is also the day after a sale that already priced a tree.

If last year's tree still stands, you are done. Friday is not a reason to buy a second one.

## The $49.98 window already closed

Home Depot's October 29, 2026 release put a 6.5-foot LED pre-lit artificial tree at $49.98. The same release put 100-count LED string lights as low as $5.98. Both sat inside a sale that ran November 5 through 25. The company said selection and price can vary by store and online. By Friday, that tag is not the plan.

9to5Toys, reading Home Depot's month of deals on November 4, said the Holiday Tree Lot opens November 27. That is the day for a real tree, if you do not already have one. It is not a second chance at the pre-lit price.

> Remember: A tree you already own is the tree.

A fresh one "for the smell" is a second tree. If it still stands and the lights still come on, stay home.

## What a real one costs

The Real Christmas Tree Board said on September 23, 2026 that 91% of 47 wholesale growers do not plan to raise wholesale prices, up from 84% in 2025. Those growers supply about half to two-thirds of the country's real trees. 85% called this year's supply strong or very strong. The price on your lot is set by the retailer. The release does not print a national average.

USA Today, updated November 12, 2026, put Home Depot real trees at $60 to $185, and said most of them land in the $60 to $100 range. Lowe's was $60 to $165. The Walmart section of that article listed $75 to $220. The opening list on the same page also said Walmart starts at $59.99. If the tag in front of you and a headline disagree, the tag wins.

KETV in Omaha, updated November 20, priced three farms that open Thanksgiving weekend:

- Frosty Pines: every tree is $60. Open November 27, 28, and 29, then weekends through December 20.
- Dappen Tree Farm in Council Bluffs: $14 per foot, plus tax. Seven feet is $98 before tax.
- Nordstrom's: $16 per foot. Seven feet is $112. Nine feet is $144.

> Truth: Wholesale holding steady is not the price on the tag.

## One height before you leave

Measure the stand you already own. The height printed on it is the height. A tree that does not fit that stand is a new stand, and that is a different purchase.

Then write one number next to the height. $60 if you are at a flat-price lot. Or the per-foot total you already multiplied. Seven times $16 is $112. That is the ceiling. A fuller tree at the same farm is how $112 becomes $144. The $185 end of the Home Depot range is a different purchase from the $60 to $100 bunch.

> Say: "Seven feet. Sixty dollars. That's the one."

> To your kid: "We pick one that fits. Then we go home."

If the $60 trees are picked over, you do not owe the upgrade. A shorter tree that fits the stand is still Christmas. Sold out is not a new number. The longer version of that no is in [explaining we can't go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go).

## The lot is not a second cart

Lights you already own are this year's lights. The $5.98 box was inside the November 5 through 25 sale. A new strand because you are in the parking lot is how one tree becomes a cart.

Same shape as [the list is one name](/guides/the-list-is-one-name). The toy ad is a different trip. Do not tour both on Friday. A small yes on the way to the car is the [dad tax](/guides/the-dad-tax): the tree was the plan, and the wreath was the bill.

USA Today also said some Home Depot real trees could be delivered, sometimes free, from November 19 through 23. That window is already over. Friday is the lot, or it is the closet.

> Truth: The taller one is a second tree.

> Say: "We've got lights. We're getting the tree."

## What you tell them

The lot is a menu. You are taking one tree home.

> To your kid: "That one is past the number."

You do not owe a speech about wholesale prices. This is the same shape as [the calendar is two dollars](/guides/the-calendar-is-two-dollars) and [the book fair is one number](/guides/the-book-fair-is-one-number). One number, then you leave. The longer version is in [talking to kids about money](/guides/talking-to-kids-about-money).

## Before you leave

- Closet first. If last year's tree still stands, stay home.
- Write the height the stand can hold.
- Write one price. A flat $60, or the per-foot total you already did.
- Lights you own stay in the bin. A wreath is a different yes.
- One lot. Not the farm and then the store to compare.

> Say: "One tree. Then we're done."

## The point

Broke does not mean broken. A $60 tree in the stand you already own is Christmas. The second height is how a Friday errand becomes $144.

> Remember: Write the height, then the price.
