Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. The stores already posted the dinner. You do not need a Pinterest menu and a second stop.

Walmart's basket is about $35 for ten. Aldi's list is under $39 for ten. Target's meal is $30 for six. Pick the list that matches the table, then close it.

## Pick the list that matches the headcount

Walmart said on October 28, 2026 that its Holiday Meal Basket is 26 items for ten people, with a Butterball turkey at 97 cents a pound. The $35 is an estimate. The footnote uses a 13.5 pound bird and about 3.2 pounds of sweet potatoes at 88 cents a pound. A 16 pound bird moves the total a few dollars, not a second grocery trip. Prices run October 28 through December 24. The deal skips Alaska, Hawaii, Oklahoma, Puerto Rico, and Wisconsin.

That $35 is the in-store number. Pickup or delivery can add a fee, from nothing up to $19.95.

> Truth: The $35 holds in the store. Delivery can add up to $19.95.

Aldi said on October 14 that a classic meal for ten is under $39, no coupon and no membership. The list is a 16-pound Farmer's Choice turkey, stuffing, sweet potatoes, rolls, cranberries, peas, carrots, celery, and pumpkin pie ingredients. It runs through November 25. The turkey starts November 4. Pie mix starts November 11. One trip on or after November 11 gets the whole list.

Target said on October 22 that its meal is $30 before tax for six, from November 8 through November 29, not in Alaska or Hawaii. The list is a Butterball up to 10 pounds, potatoes, cranberry sauce, the green bean casserole cans, stuffing, gravy, and two baguettes. There is no pie in that $30.

> Remember: The headcount picks the list. One list is the dinner.

A house of four does not need the ten-person basket and another store. Target's six is the closer fit, and you will still have leftovers. A house that is actually feeding about ten uses Walmart or Aldi and stops.

## $90 is an open list

PwC told ABC News, in a forecast shared around October 21, that a Thanksgiving meal might land near $90, with turkey up nearly 8% from last year. That is a private guess. PwC did not publish the item list behind it. The American Farm Bureau's 2025 survey put a classic dinner for ten at $55.18. Those two numbers are not the same meal, and neither one is a receipt.

The receipt you can use in the aisle is the posted basket.

> Truth: $90 is what an open list costs. $35 is a closed one.

> Say: "We're doing the $35 basket. That's the dinner."

If someone wants to bring a dish, one dish is a gift. A second menu is a second bill.

> To the group text: "We've got the basket. Bring one thing if you want."

## Do not shop the dinner twice

The basket already has stuffing, potatoes, a vegetable, and, at Walmart or Aldi, a pie. The add-on aisle is a relish tray, soda, and "the kids will want something else." That pile is how a posted price turns into the forecast.

Write the store on the fridge before you park. If the bird is not in yet, wait for the day the list is actually the list. Do not pre-build a cart of stand-ins and then buy the basket too.

> Say: "One store. One list. I'm not doing a second cart."

## What you tell the kids

They will hear about someone else's three pies. You do not owe a speech about money.

> To your kid: "We're cooking the basket. It already has pie."

If the cousins are not coming, say that in one line. A smaller table is Target's meal for six. It is still Thanksgiving.

> To your kid: "It's just us this year. We're still doing pie."

## Do it before the bird is in

Pick the store that matches the headcount, and text it before the group assigns you a second protein.

- About ten people, and your state is on Walmart's list: the $35 basket, in the store if you can.
- About ten people at Aldi: under $39, on or after November 11, when the turkey and the pie mix are both in the deal.
- About six: Target's $30, from November 8. If you want pie, buy one pie, not a second dinner.

> Say: "I'm getting the basket. Don't add a second dinner to the list."

## The point

Broke does not mean broken. Ten people at about $35, or six at $30, is a real Thursday. The second cart is how a posted dinner becomes the $90 version nobody meant to ring up.

> Remember: Close the list before you park.
