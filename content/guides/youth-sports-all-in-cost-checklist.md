The flyer said $85. Your gut said maybe. Your calendar said September is already loud.

The longer story is in [the sports signup fee you did not budget for](/guides/the-sports-signup-fee-you-didnt-budget-for). This page is the clipboard: an all-in checklist you can run in ten minutes before anyone hits pay.

## The checklist (write real numbers)

**1. Registration / league fee**  
The number on the email.

**2. Required uniform and gear**  
Cleats, pads, specialty socks, bag. Ask what is mandatory vs "looks cool."

**3. Travel**  
Count likely tournaments. Gas, food, maybe a hotel. One "away" weekend can beat the signup fee.

**4. Photos and keepsakes**  
Optional until the packet makes your kid the exception.

**5. Volunteer hours or buyout**  
Time you do not have, converted to cash.

**6. Team fees and snack duty**  
Pizza nights, coach gifts, end-of-season party.

**7. Misc buffer**  
Mouthguard, spirit wear, replacement ball. Put $40-$75 here on purpose so it is not a surprise. That buffer is [dad tax](/guides/the-dad-tax) with a jersey on.

Add them. That sum is the decision, not line one.

> Truth: If you cannot write a number for travel, you are not ready to say yes.

## Three emails that save you

Send one message:

1. What did most families spend **all-in** last season?
2. What is **required** vs optional?
3. Is there **scholarship, loaner gear, or a payment plan**?

Silence is an answer. A range is better than a vibe.

This sits in the same season as [the second bill](/guides/the-second-bill) after school starts. August and September like to invoice twice.

## Decide out loud

**Yes:** you can name the payday that covers it without skipping rent groceries.

**Not this season:** short script, one alternative (school ball, pickup, a different cheaper league).

> Say: "Not this season. We are covering practices at the park."

> To your kid: "We priced the whole thing. This year is different."

More tone: [explaining we cannot go](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go).

## The point

Kids deserve sports when the household can carry them. The checklist does not make you cold. It makes you the adult who refuses to call the trailer the movie. Click pay only after the all-in number has a place to live.
