The registration fee is the number they underline. It is rarely the number that hits your week.

Parents know this in their bones. Surveys keep confirming it: sports still matter, and the bill keeps growing in the quiet corners. Gate fees. Gas. The third "team snack" week. A tournament hotel that was "probably optional" until it was not.

If you already survived August's fee pile, [the second bill after school starts](/guides/the-second-bill) is the cousin of this piece. This one is the sports-shaped version.

## Write the real season card

Before you sign, make a ugly little card:

1. **Registration / club dues** (the form number)
2. **Kit** (uniform, cleats, the "required" jacket)
3. **Weekly gas** (practice + games, honest miles)
4. **Gate / player fees** (cash at the door adds up)
5. **Team food tax** (snacks, end-of-season party, coach gift)
6. **Tournament pad** (one overnight or three "day trips")

Add them. If the total makes your stomach drop, believe your stomach. The form did not lie. It just left out the plot.

## The yes that stays honest

**Local before travel.** Rec and school ball are not a moral failure. They are how a lot of good kids still get a team without a second rent payment.

**One sport per season if money is loud.** Two sports is two snack schedules and two gas maps.

**Name the cut when you say yes.** "We're in for fall soccer. We're out for the holiday showcase." Kids can handle a boundary. They cannot handle a surprise hotel they hear about in a group text.

**Ask for last season's all-in number.** A coach who gets quiet when you ask is giving you information.

## Scripts for the parking lot

- "Registration is one line. What's the all-in from last year?"
- "We can do local season. Travel is a no this year."
- "Team snack week is fine. Hotel weekends are not in the plan."
- "We're not quitting sports. We're quitting surprise invoices."

You do not need a manifesto about inequality at the snack table. You need a plan that survives October.

## The point

Sports can still be worth it. Worth it includes gas, gate, and the snack rotation, not just the bold number on page one. Broke does not mean broken. It means "yes" to the season is a full-sentence yes, with the hidden fees written where you can see them.
