Date night dies in the logistics. The second you start pricing a sitter, parking, and a restaurant that takes adults seriously, the couch wins.

Keep the kids. Change the rules. Same calm energy as [explaining “we can’t go”](/guides/explaining-we-cant-go): one honest plan beats an expensive apology.

## The 90-minute home date

1. **Announce it.** Tell the kids: "After dinner, it's grown-up time. You get a movie / extra story / the cheap popcorn. We get the table."
2. **Close the kitchen.** Paper plates if that is what it takes. No loading the dishwasher "real quick."
3. **Eat something you would not eat on a Tuesday.** Frozen pizza dressed up with extra cheese still counts if you light a candle and put your phones in a bowl.
4. **Ask one question that is not about the calendar.** "What did you used to want to be?" is better than "Did you email the preschool?"

## If you can leave the house for $20

Walk a grocery store like it is a museum. Split a bakery item in the parking lot. Drive to the place with the good sunset and sit in the car like teenagers with a better credit score (still not great).

The point is not the venue. The point is a slice of time that is not a staff meeting about children.

## What not to do

Do not turn it into a budget lecture. Do not "use the time" to talk about the leaking faucet. Put the faucet on a note for Sunday. Tonight you are two people who picked each other, even if the baby monitor is on the table.
