Black Friday is not one night. Walmart posted three holiday sales that run from November 12 through December 2. The first one already ended on November 15. The next one opens November 23. Cyber Monday is November 30 through December 2.

Three openings is how a $5 game becomes a TV. You do not need a plan for all three. You need one name.

## The $5 game is a whole gift

Mama Cheaps checked Walmart.com on November 12, 2026, during the early sale. Yahtzee Classic was $5. Candy Land was $5. Two Fisher-Price Little People sets, a kayak and a fishing trip, were $5 each. LEGO DUPLO My First Animal Train, set 10412, was $10. A 10-inch Star Wars Grogu toy was $15. A six-pack Spidey bundle was $20. A Toniebox 2 Sesame Street bundle was $119.

USA Today, the same day, listed a preschool ice-block game at $5, a 46-piece Magna-Tiles set at $20, and a 50-inch Vizio at $128.

Walmart's October 26 release says the season has thousands of gifts under $20, and 25 toys on the Top Toys list under $25.

A $5 Yahtzee is a present. A $128 TV is a different purchase on the same page. So is the $119 bundle.

> Remember: One name is the list.

Write the name before you open the site. One kid, one name. Two kids, two names, still one each. The page will offer a scooter and a TV under the games. Those are not the name you wrote.

> Say: "We're getting Yahtzee. That's the one."

If Yahtzee is gone by November 23, the job does not upgrade. Candy Land at $5, or whatever $5 game is still on the shelf, is the same job. Sold out is not a reason to buy the Toniebox.

## Three dates are one trap

Walmart's October 26, 2026 release splits the season like this.

Early Black Friday is November 12-15. Online starts at 12:01 a.m. ET. Stores start at 6 a.m. local time. Walmart+ hot drops start November 12. Whatever is left opens to everyone at 12:01 a.m. ET on November 13.

Black Friday deals are November 23-29. Online starts November 23 at 12:01 a.m. ET. In stores, the deals start November 27 at 6 a.m. local time. Walmart+ hot drops start November 23. Leftovers open to everyone on November 24.

Cyber Monday is November 30 through December 2, online only, starting at 12:01 a.m. ET.

If the name is already in a bag from November 12-15, you are done. Do not tour November 23 to see what else is cheap. If you missed the first window, November 23-29 is the one trip. Not a second pass on November 30.

> Truth: A second event is a second cart.

That small yes is the [dad tax](/guides/the-dad-tax). A $5 game is the plan. "While I'm here" is the bill.

## Do not join a club for a board game

USA Today on October 4, 2026 put Walmart+ at $12.95 a month or $98 a year, plus tax. Walmart's October 26 release adds a holiday bundle: Walmart+ and Sam's Club Plus together for $120 a year. It auto-renews.

Members get a head start on hot drops, about 24 hours, and some online-only deals stay member-only for the first day while supplies last. That matters if you already pay for the membership because the groceries show up at the door. It does not matter for Yahtzee.

A $98 fee to be first in line for a $5 game is the purchase. The game is not.

> Say: "I'm not joining a club for a board game."

## What you tell the kids

The ad is a menu. You are not taking the whole menu home.

> To your kid: "Pick one. We'll get that one."

If the name they want is past the number on your note, say that. You do not owe a speech about three sale dates. The longer version is in [talking to kids about money](/guides/talking-to-kids-about-money). Same shape as [the book fair is one number](/guides/the-book-fair-is-one-number) and [the calendar is two dollars](/guides/the-calendar-is-two-dollars). This one is the sale.

> To your kid: "That one's past the number."

> Truth: The sale does not get a second name.

## Before you open the tab

- Write one name. A $5 game, a $10 DUPLO set, or a toy you already picked under $20.
- Bought it November 12-15: stop.
- Not bought yet: one order in the November 23-29 window. Cyber Monday does not get a new name.
- The $128 TV, the $119 bundle, and a new $98 membership stay out of this cart.
- Two kids means two names. It does not mean two stores.

> Say: "One name. Then I'm done."

## The point

Broke does not mean broken. A $5 game under the tree is a real gift. The second sale is how a list with one name becomes the cart you did not mean to fill.

> Remember: Write the name, then close the tab.
