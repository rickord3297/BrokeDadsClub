# Dispatch #02: short-form video scripts

Source: `content/newsletter/002-youth-sports-gear-cutoff-and-bare-tool-pricing.md`
Format: direct to camera, vertical 9:16, TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels. One take is fine. Talk like you are telling a buddy at the sideline, not presenting.

Timing assumes about 150 spoken words per minute. Prices are ranges on purpose: say "about" and "usually" out loud. Do not name a brand price you have not checked this week.

---

## Script 1: "Stop buying the combo kit"

**Length:** about 35 seconds (90 words)
**Angle:** bare tools vs combo kits

| Time | Say | On screen |
|------|-----|-----------|
| 0-3s | "If you own one cordless drill, stop buying tool kits." | Text: STOP BUYING THE KIT |
| 3-12s | "The kit price looks like a deal because it comes with a battery and a charger. You already own those. You're paying for them again." | Hold up a battery. Text: YOU ALREADY OWN THIS |
| 12-24s | "Look for two words on the tag: tool only. Same tool, no battery, no charger. Usually a big chunk cheaper." | Close-up of a shelf tag, circle "tool only" |
| 24-32s | "Pick one battery brand. Stay on it. Every tool after the first one, buy bare." | Text: ONE PLATFORM. BUY BARE. |
| 32-35s | "I break down one thing like this every Sunday. It's free, link's in my bio." | Point down. Text: WEEKLY BREAKDOWN, LINK IN BIO |

**Caption:** The most expensive part of every cordless tool is the battery you already own. #dadtips #frugalliving #powertools

## Script 2: "Don't buy the gear before practice one"

**Length:** about 40 seconds (100 words)
**Angle:** buying youth sports gear too early

| Time | Say | On screen |
|------|-----|-----------|
| 0-3s | "Your kid hasn't been to practice yet. Why do they own a hundred bucks of gear?" | Text: PRACTICE HASN'T STARTED |
| 3-13s | "Here's what we do now. Before the season, two numbers. A dollar cap for gear. And a date, about two weeks in." | Write "$ cap" and a date on a sticky note |
| 13-25s | "Until that date, we borrow and make do. Cleats and shin guards, that's it. Kids quit things. That's not a parenting failure, that's being eight." | Text: BORROW UNTIL WEEK 2 |
| 25-35s | "Still excited after two weeks? Now we buy the real bag, the second ball, the stuff." | Stick the note on the fridge |
| 35-40s | "One tactic like this every Sunday. Free. Link in bio." | Text: SUNDAY BREAKDOWN, LINK IN BIO |

**Caption:** Set the gear cutoff before the season sets it for you. #youthsports #soccerdad #dadlife

## Script 3: "The water you buy every Saturday"

**Length:** about 35 seconds (90 words)
**Angle:** small repeat purchases vs one buy

| Time | Say | On screen |
|------|-----|-----------|
| 0-3s | "You're buying a case of water every game day. Do the math with me." | Text: THE SIDELINE TAX |
| 3-14s | "A case is about five or six bucks. A fall season is ten, twelve Saturdays. That's fifty, sixty dollars of water." | On-screen math: $5.50 x 11 = $60 |
| 14-25s | "One big insulated jug is usually under twenty-five. You fill it at home. It's cold all game. Done." | Set the jug down next to a folding chair |
| 25-31s | "Get one with a handle you can carry with the chair in your other hand. That's the whole review." | Carry both. Text: ONE TRIP FROM THE CAR |
| 31-35s | "I do this math every Sunday. Weekly breakdown's free, link in bio." | Text: LINK IN BIO |

**Caption:** One jug beats a season of cases. #frugaltips #sportsparent #dadmath

## Notes before filming

- Hook line goes first, no intro, no "hey guys." The first word should be part of the challenge.
- Keep the CTA to one sentence. "Weekly breakdown" is the promise. Do not say "newsletter."
- If a platform blocks the word "link," swap to "it's in my profile."
- No affiliate links in captions. The bio link goes to brokedadsclub.com/newsletter.
