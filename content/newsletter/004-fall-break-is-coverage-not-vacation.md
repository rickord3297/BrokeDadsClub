Sunday night before fall break is when the week gets invented in the driveway. School is closed. Work is not. The after-school program that usually covers Tuesday often shuts down with the calendar. That is the scramble.

This week's tactic: **treat fall break as a coverage week, not a vacation**, unless your job closed too. Open the district calendar and write the real dates. Then get **one quote** from a place you would actually use. A city day camp in Elizabeth, Colorado posted $55 a day for October 12 to 16. A Memphis camp posted $195 for five days. Metro Atlanta day programs landed around $60 to $125 a day. Your town will not match those numbers. Call yours.

Three lanes. Days a program covers. Days a person you trust covers. Days someone takes off work. Put a name on every weekday. The [week school closes and work does not](/guides/the-week-school-closes-and-work-does-not) walkthrough has the scripts. Print the [fall break coverage plan](/resources/fall-break-coverage-plan) if you want it on the fridge.

This week, before Sunday night: write the closed dates, price one option, and fill the blank days. A blank day does not get nicer by waiting.

See you next Sunday.
