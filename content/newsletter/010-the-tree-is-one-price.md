Sunday at 1:20 p.m. the lot smells like pine and your kid is already pointing at a nine-foot tree. Nobody wrote a number down before getting in the car, so the tallest one in the row is about to win.

This week's tactic: **one height and one price.** Check the closet first. If last year's tree still stands and the lights come on, stay home. Home Depot's **$49.98** pre-lit sale closed November 25, so the lot opening is not a second shot at that price.

If you are buying real, do the math before you park. USA Today put most Home Depot real trees at **$60 to $100**, with the full span reaching **$185**. KETV in Omaha priced one flat-rate farm at **$60** for any tree and two per-foot farms at **$14** and **$16** a foot. Seven feet at $16 is **$112**. Nine feet is **$144**. Measure the stand you already own, cap the height to fit it, and leave the parking-lot lights and wreaths alone.
