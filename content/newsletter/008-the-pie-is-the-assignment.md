Tuesday at 8:05 p.m. the group text lands: "Just bring dessert." Then someone adds "and maybe a side if you can." Nobody named a price, so the cart is about to grow a casserole you did not plan.

This week's tactic: **one pie is the assignment.** MoneyLion's Costco roundup on November 6 put the Kirkland Signature pumpkin pie at **$5.99** in the warehouse (58 ounces, about 12 slices) and **$6.80** for same-day delivery. Walmart.com the next day listed an 8-inch, 22-ounce Freshness Guaranteed pumpkin pie at **$4.94**, down from $5.93. Buy one of those shapes. Not both. Not a pie plus the green beans you bake so a store crust does not look lazy.

The same Costco list had a Complete Gourmet Thanksgiving Turkey Dinner at **$199.99** for about eight people, with two pies already inside. That box is a hosted meal. You were asked for dessert. A $6 pie in the cart is how "while I'm here" becomes $200. Text the line now: one pumpkin pie, nothing else. Buy it Tuesday or Wednesday of Thanksgiving week, when the case is stocked. Costco is closed on Thanksgiving Day, so Wednesday is the last grab. The [pie is the assignment](/guides/the-pie-is-the-assignment) walkthrough has the short lines for the group text and for the kids.

This week: send "We've got dessert. One pumpkin pie." Leave the side off your name. The regular grocery week still has to happen.

See you next Sunday.
