Monday at 7:50 a.m. the backpack is already zipped and someone in the group text just posted a link to a teacher tumbler. A lot of schools send kids home early on Wednesday, November 25. The thread is shopping for a gift holiday that is not on the calendar.

This week's tactic: **the card is the gift.** USA Today on November 14 framed **$15 to $30** as a useful range for a holiday teacher gift, and listed DoorDash eGift cards from **$5**, Amazon cards from **$10**, and an Owala FreeSip on the same page. Walmart.com that day had the FreeSip 24 oz at **$29.97**. Five dollars is a card. Thirty dollars is a second Christmas. Thanksgiving is not a teacher-gift holiday. The note is.

You do not need a boxed set. Folded notebook paper is enough. Ask for one sentence. Write the words they say if they freeze, and let them sign it. Put it in the backpack Sunday or Monday. A card that misses Wednesday becomes the December note, not a tumbler order tonight. If a room parent is already collecting for December, that collection is the gift. Reply once, then stop. The [card is the gift](/guides/the-card-is-the-gift) walkthrough has the short lines for your kid and for the text thread.

This week: one line on paper, in the bag before the early day. Leave the tumbler in the tab.

See you next Sunday.
